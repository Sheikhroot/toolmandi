Age Calculator

Enter a date of birth — get the exact age in years, months and days, the way forms ask for it.

Why an exact age matters

Job applications, NADRA records, exam forms and government eligibility rules often ask for age in exact years, months and days — sometimes "as on" a specific closing date. Guessing a year and subtracting is how applications get rejected. Set the second date to the form's closing date and this calculator gives you the precise answer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the age calculated?

Exactly the way official forms do: full years from the birth date, then full months, then the remaining days — leap years included.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. The calculation happens in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
