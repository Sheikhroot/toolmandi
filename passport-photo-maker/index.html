How to make a passport size photo

  1. Take or choose a front-facing photo — face in the centre, plain light background, no cap or sunglasses.
  2. Choose the size your form needs: 35 × 45 mm for passports and most visas, 40 × 60 mm for Pakistani CNIC, 2 × 2 inch for US visa.
  3. Drag the photo and use the zoom slider until your face fills the frame the way passport photos do — head and shoulders, face clearly visible.
  4. Download the single photo, or a print sheet you can print once and cut many copies from.

Need the photo under a KB limit as well?

Many online forms also limit the file size (for example 50 KB or 100 KB). After making your photo here, run it through our Compress Image to KB tool to hit the exact size.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the passport photo size in Pakistan?

Pakistani passport and most visa photos are 35 × 45 mm. CNIC photos commonly use 40 × 60 mm. Always confirm the size written on your form or portal.

Can this tool remove or change the background?

No — and be careful of tools that promise it carelessly, because a badly cut background is a common reason photos get rejected. Shoot against a plain white or light wall instead; this tool then crops you to the exact official size.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Everything happens inside your browser on your own device.
