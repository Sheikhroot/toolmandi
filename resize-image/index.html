How to resize an image

  1. Choose your photo and read its current size.
  2. Pick a preset or type the exact width and height you need. Keep "Keep ratio" on to avoid stretching — turn it off only when a form demands exact pixels (like PPSC 300×300).
  3. Press Resize & Download.

If the portal also limits the file size in KB, finish with our Compress Image to KB tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

My photo looks stretched — why?

That happens when the new width and height have a different ratio than the original and "Keep ratio" is off. Only do that when a form explicitly demands exact dimensions.

Is my image uploaded?

No — resizing runs entirely in your browser.
