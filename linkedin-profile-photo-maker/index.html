The right LinkedIn profile photo size

LinkedIn asks for a square photo of at least 400 × 400 pixels and displays it as a circle. This tool exports 800 × 800 — double the minimum, so your photo stays sharp on every screen. Frame your face to fill roughly 60% of the circle, keep some space above your head, and smile the way you would meeting a client.

Quick wins recruiters notice

Plain background, natural light from the front, no sunglasses or group-photo crops, and clothes you would wear to a meeting. Profiles with a clear, professional photo get dramatically more views and connection accepts than logos, selfies in cars, or cropped wedding photos.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the download come as a square if LinkedIn shows a circle?

LinkedIn itself crops your square upload into the circle. The gold circle in this tool previews that crop exactly, so there are no surprises after uploading.

Does the background change work on any photo?

Best on a plain background. If your photo already has a decent background, keep the original — natural is professional.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Everything runs inside your browser on your device.
