Good ways to use a QR code

On your shop counter (link it to your WhatsApp or your shop page), on packaging and labels (link to your page or catalogue), on business cards and flyers, and on restaurant tables for the menu. Pair it with our WhatsApp Link Generator to make a QR that opens a chat with you.

Print tip

Print QR codes at least 2.5 cm wide, keep the quiet white border around them, and always test-scan the printed copy before printing a full batch.
