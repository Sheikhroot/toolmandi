Why ToolMandi?

Most online tools upload your photo or documents to a server you know nothing about. ToolMandi is different: every tool runs inside your own browser. Your CNIC photo, signature and documents never leave your phone or computer — so there is nothing to leak, nothing to misuse, and no waiting for slow uploads.

Made for Pakistani forms

Government job portals, exam forms and university admissions in Pakistan ask for photos in exact sizes — FPSC wants the photo under 50–100 KB, PPSC asks 300×300 pixels, NTS wants 30–60 KB, and signatures are often limited to 10–20 KB. Our compress tool carries these presets built in, so one tap sets everything correctly.

Free, really

No signup, no watermark, no daily limit games. Because the tools run on your device, they cost almost nothing to run — and that saving is passed to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos uploaded to your server?

No. All processing happens in your browser on your own device. You can even disconnect the internet after the page loads and the tools will still work.

Is ToolMandi free?

Yes — every tool is free, with no signup and no watermark.

Which photo size do Pakistani job portals want?

It differs by portal: FPSC/CSS photos are typically 50–100 KB, PPSC 300×300 px under 100 KB, NTS 30–60 KB, MDCAT/ECAT around 100 KB, and signatures 10–20 KB. Always confirm the exact requirement written on your form, then use the matching preset in our Compress Image to KB tool.
