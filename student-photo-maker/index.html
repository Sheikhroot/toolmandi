How to make a student ID photo

  1. Take a front-facing photo against the plainest wall you can find, in good light.
  2. Choose the size your school, college or form asks for — 35 × 45 mm is the most common.
  3. Drag and zoom until your head and shoulders fill the frame.
  4. Set the background to white (or blue, when your institution asks for blue) and adjust the strength until the edges look clean.
  5. Download the photo — or a print sheet with many copies for forms that need several.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which background do institutions want — white or blue?

Most Pakistani schools, colleges and exam boards accept white; some ask for blue. Always follow the instruction written on your form — both are one tap here.

Will the background change work on any photo?

It works best when the original background is plain (a wall). Busy backgrounds with many colours close to your clothes or skin can fool it — in that case, retake the photo against a plain wall; it takes a minute and gives a studio-like result.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Everything runs inside your browser on your device.
