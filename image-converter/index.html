Which format should you choose?

JPG — the safe choice for photos and almost every online form. Smallest files, accepted everywhere, but no transparency. PNG — best for logos, signatures and screenshots; keeps transparent backgrounds, but files are bigger. WebP — the modern web format, small like JPG with transparency like PNG; great for websites, but some old portals do not accept it.

Converting a transparent PNG to JPG fills the background with white automatically.
