How to compress an image to an exact KB size

  1. Choose your photo — a passport photo, CNIC scan or signature.
  2. Pick a preset (FPSC 50 KB, NTS 60 KB, PPSC/MDCAT 100 KB, signature 20 KB) or type your own target size.
  3. Press Compress Photo. The tool automatically finds the highest quality that still fits under your target, and reduces the photo's dimensions only if that is the only way to fit.
  4. Download the result and upload it to your form.

Common Pakistani form photo sizes

FPSC / CSS photos are typically required at 50–100 KB, PPSC at 300×300 px under 100 KB, NTS at 30–60 KB, MDCAT / ECAT at about 100 KB with signatures up to 200 KB, while most portals limit a signature image to 10–20 KB. Requirements change — always read the exact line on your own form first, then choose the matching preset above.

Why does the output become a JPG?

Almost every government and exam portal accepts JPG, and JPG is the only common format whose file size can be tuned precisely to a KB target. If your photo is a PNG, it is converted to JPG on a white background automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Compression runs in your browser using your own device. Nothing is sent to a server.

The portal still rejects my photo — why?

Some portals also fix the pixel dimensions (for example 300×300). Use our Resize Image tool to set the exact pixels first, then compress here.

Can I compress a signature to 20 KB?

Yes — choose the Signature preset. For a clean result, sign with a dark pen on plain white paper and photograph it in good light.
