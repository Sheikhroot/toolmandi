YouTube Thumbnail Downloader

Paste a YouTube video link — get its thumbnail in every size, ready to download.

How it works

Every public YouTube video has official thumbnail images stored by YouTube itself at predictable addresses. This tool simply reads the video ID from your link and shows you those images — the HD version (1280×720) when the channel uploaded one, plus SD and smaller sizes.

A note on use

Thumbnails belong to their creators. Download them for study, inspiration and fair reference — do not re-upload someone else's thumbnail as your own.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the HD thumbnail missing for some videos?

Older videos and some Shorts never had a 1280×720 thumbnail. In that case the SD (640×480) version is the best available.

Does this download videos?

No — only the thumbnail image. This tool does not download videos.
