How to merge PDF files

  1. Add your PDF files — tap again to add more.
  2. Use the ↑ ↓ buttons to arrange them in the order you want in the final file.
  3. Press Merge PDFs and download the combined file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my documents uploaded to a server?

No. This is the biggest difference from most merge tools: the files are combined inside your browser, on your own device. Personal documents — CNIC, income papers, applications — never travel anywhere.

Is there a file limit?

There is no limit set by us. Very large files depend on your device's memory — a normal phone or computer handles typical application documents easily.
